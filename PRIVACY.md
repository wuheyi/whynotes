# whynotes Privacy Policy

whynotes is designed as a local-first macOS todo and memo app.

## Data Storage

Your todos, memos, attachments, settings, and API key are stored locally on your Mac. whynotes does not create an account system and does not operate a server to collect your personal data.

## AI Features

If you enable AI features, the text you enter for AI generation or editing may be sent to the configured model provider, such as DeepSeek, only for the purpose of generating or modifying your todo content.

Your DeepSeek API key is stored locally on your device. whynotes does not send your API key to the developer.

## Attachments

Images and files added to whynotes are stored locally with your notes. If you include attachments in an AI request, relevant content may be processed by the configured AI provider.

## Analytics and Tracking

whynotes does not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
